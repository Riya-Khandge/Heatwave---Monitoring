About Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The Heatwave Intelligence System helps monitor heatwave conditions and provides early warning alerts to protect citizens.


Data Sources

Data is collected from AWS sensors, IMD databases, and satellite observations.


Stakeholders

Government agencies, disaster management teams, hospitals, and the general public use this system.


"Early warnings save lives. Timely weather information helps communities prepare and reduce the impact of severe heatwaves."

— Chief Meteorologist
